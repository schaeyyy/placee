Mt. Oro

Location

San Isidro, Rodriguez, Rizal

Overview

A prominent peak in Rodriguez, Rizal celebrated by hikers for its sea of clouds and sunrise trail ridge views.

Highlights

  • Sea of clouds
  • Sunrise views
  • Trail ridge views

Montalban Sports Complex

Location

San Jose, Rodriguez, Rizal

Overview

Home to the Ynares Center arena, serving as the central hub for local youth athletic training and community gatherings.

Highlights

  • Ynares Center arena
  • Youth athletic training

San Jose Manggagawa Parish

Location

San Jose, Rodriguez, Rizal

Overview

Established in Barangay San Jose under the Diocese of Antipolo, acting as a spiritual core driven by active community livelihood programs.

Highlights

  • Diocese of Antipolo
  • Spiritual core
  • Livelihood programs

Metro Manila Hills

Location

San Jose, Rodriguez, Rizal

Overview

An elevated, flood-free residential community nestled along the Sierra Madre foothills representing modern urban extension.

Highlights

  • Elevated
  • Flood-free
  • Modern urban extension
